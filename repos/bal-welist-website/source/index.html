Will Executor Server List

This page displays all Will Executor servers that manage the inheritance processing for the Bitcoin After Life plugin on Electrum. Will Executors operate in a competitive environment, racing to broadcast inheritance transactions to network nodes and earn the associated transaction fees.

How ranking works

Ordered by total deposited, then amount donated, then WeTime points, then base fee (lower first).

Yearly fee50,000 sats

Renewalautomatic from your credit

Paying more moves your server up: the total deposited counts first and never decays, and the amount donated counts second. Points are the third criterion and the base fee only breaks ties. Details in the Terms →

The fee column shows the base fee each executor advertises. Executors may also publish a daily fee: the minimum they charge is the higher of the base fee and the daily fee multiplied by the remaining days of the inheritance. The base fee is the last tie-breaker in the order; the daily fee does not affect it. Who charges what? →

01

Benefits of Listing

Listed Will Executors are automatically integrated into the plugin when inheritance arrangements are made, providing direct access to potential clients.

02

Ranking System

This leaderboard is ordered by total deposited, then amount donated, then WeTime points, then lower base fee; if all four are equal, the server id keeps the order stable.

03

Listing & Renewal

A flat yearly fee buys 365 days of presence in the list, starting when your payment reaches 3 confirmations. Anything you deposit above one period stays as credit: when a period ends, the days that credit can pay for are granted automatically, with no renewal window. When the credit cannot pay for another day, the server leaves the list. See the Terms for details.

04

Revenue Expectations

Operating as a Will Executor is not a get-rich-quick opportunity. This service requires patience and long-term commitment, but can provide substantial returns over time for dedicated operators.
